## What should keep running

Until you have good reason to change them, try to preserve:

- our main phone numbers and internet;
- essential utilities;
- health, home, vehicle, and other important insurance;
- payments connected to our home and our daughter;
- my main email and protected-information recovery routes; and
- anything an adviser or provider says must remain active.

## What can usually wait

Unless safety, a deadline, or qualified advice says otherwise, household tidying, most subscriptions, long-term account closures, possessions, and nonessential projects can wait. Unfamiliar does not always mean urgent.
