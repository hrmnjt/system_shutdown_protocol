## Start with people, not administration

- [ ] Contact **[primary trusted person, relationship, phone]**.
- [ ] Contact **[second trusted person, relationship, phone]**.
- [ ] Make sure dependants and pets are safe and cared for.
- [ ] Ask someone trusted to stay with or regularly check on the immediate family.

## Follow local procedures

The correct medical, legal, and registration process depends on where and how the death occurred. Contact **[doctor, hospital, local authority, funeral provider, or other known adviser]** and follow their instructions.

- [ ] Obtain professional guidance before moving, signing, or disposing of anything important.
- [ ] Notify the executor or intended responsible person: **[name and phone]**.
- [ ] Locate identification and relevant medical documents at **[location]**.

## Secure immediate responsibilities

Only do what is needed to prevent harm or loss.

- [ ] Ensure the home, vehicle, and work equipment are secure.
- [ ] Arrange immediate care for **[children, elders, pets, or others]**.
- [ ] Check for travel, deliveries, or appointments in the next 48 hours.
- [ ] Keep phones and devices charged; do not reset or erase them.

## Avoid rushed decisions

Unless a professional advises otherwise, do not immediately close bank accounts, cancel phone numbers, erase devices, distribute property, or post detailed information publicly. Some of these may be needed for records, authentication, or estate administration.
