## What should keep running

Keep housing payments, essential utilities, communications, security, necessary maintenance, and vehicle insurance running until you understand the consequences of changing them.

## What can wait

Decorating, improvements, nonurgent repairs, optional services, most subscriptions, and decisions about property or possessions can wait.

## If I cannot handle this

Start with immediate safety and the next real deadline. Use **[Our calendar and recurring routines](routines.html)** rather than trying to inspect every provider at once, and ask a trusted person to take one practical task at a time.
