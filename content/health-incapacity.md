## If I am in hospital and can speak to you

Ask me what I need and what I am comfortable sharing. Please write down important decisions, names, and reference numbers because neither of us should have to rely on memory at a stressful time.

Keep my identity documents, insurance information, phone, and necessary devices safe. Check only the household, school, dog, and work commitments that cannot wait.

## If I cannot speak for myself

1. Find out where I am and which medical or official team is responsible.
2. Use the protected information below for my identity, insurance, medicines, allergies, and recent care.
3. Check **[Access, identity, and authority](authority-documents.html#what-i-intend-you-to-handle)** before assuming what you may consent to, sign, or access.
4. Tell my employer if it is relevant and ask HR for their written process.
5. Make sure our daughter and dog are cared for.
6. Keep my phone and devices charged and safe. Please do not reset them or keep guessing passwords.

I intend you to speak and coordinate for me, but I still need to verify the documents and legal position that let you make particular medical or financial decisions.

## What should keep running

Keep health insurance, necessary medicines, planned care, and access to medical records active. Preserve receipts, approvals, and reference numbers.

## What can wait

Routine appointments that a clinician says can wait, nonessential claims, and general record organization can be dealt with after immediate care and family needs.

## If this lasts for more than a short time

You may need help reviewing:

- medical and financial authority;
- your and our daughter’s UAE residency and insurance;
- my leave, salary, benefits, and company property;
- bills and responsibilities that need to continue;
- our daughter’s school, care, travel, and documents;
- access to my password manager and encrypted files; and
- whether this handbook needs an operational update.

Please ask for professional help where authority or consequences are unclear. My intended executor role for you after my death does not, by itself, settle what you can do while I am alive.
