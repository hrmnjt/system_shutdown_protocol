## Work with the responsible person

- [ ] Confirm who is legally responsible for administering the estate.
- [ ] Locate the will or other instructions at **[location]**.
- [ ] Contact **[lawyer or legal adviser, phone, email]**.
- [ ] Ask what certified records or copies will be required locally.
- [ ] Keep a written log of calls, reference numbers, expenses, and documents submitted.

## People and organizations

Use the **Key contacts** section rather than trying to remember everyone.

- [ ] Immediate family and close friends
- [ ] Employer or business partners
- [ ] Insurance adviser
- [ ] Accountant or tax adviser
- [ ] Banks and financial institutions, when the executor advises
- [ ] Landlord, housing society, or property manager, if relevant

## Ongoing household needs

- [ ] Identify bills due within the next 30 days.
- [ ] Keep essential utilities and communication services running.
- [ ] Check rent, mortgage, insurance, school, care, and medical obligations.
- [ ] Redirect important physical mail if appropriate.

## Records

Create one folder—physical or digital—for every document received. Do not give away the only original of an important document without recording who received it.
