### If I am in hospital or cannot communicate

Tell my manager or HR only what they need to know. Ask them to explain their process in writing and help you understand:

- what notification or medical evidence they need;
- leave and what happens to my work;
- health-insurance support;
- salary and employment benefits;
- my company devices or other property;
- anything that affects your or our daughter’s residency; and
- whom you should speak with next.

Write down names, reference numbers, documents requested, and dates. Please do not sign in to company systems as me or return equipment informally.

### If I have died

Ask HR, in writing where possible, about:

- the records they need from you;
- salary or other employment-related amounts;
- insurance and benefits;
- company property;
- residency and sponsorship matters; and
- the person who will stay responsible for your questions.

This handbook cannot promise what the answer will be. The employer and appropriate UAE guidance must confirm it.
