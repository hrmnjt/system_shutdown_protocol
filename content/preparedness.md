## What “Last reviewed” means

The date shown in this handbook is the last date on which I deliberately checked that the protocol was accurate, accessible, and usable. It is **not** the date the site was built or the date a file was edited.

“Not yet reviewed” means this is still a draft and must not be assumed complete.

## How to review this protocol {#how-to-review}

A review is complete only after these checks have been performed:

1. **People:** Call or message the primary contacts as appropriate and confirm their details and roles.
2. **Authority:** Confirm the will, executor, beneficiary or nominee designations, and other formal documents with qualified local advice.
3. **Documents:** Open the document locations and confirm that the expected records are present and current.
4. **Finances:** Compare the account, asset, liability, insurance, tax, and recurring-payment overview with current records.
5. **Access:** Test the password-manager emergency-access process and recovery material without exposing or copying secrets into this site.
6. **Responsibilities:** Check dependant, pet, household, employment, business, and community information.
7. **Wishes:** Re-read personal and digital wishes and resolve contradictions with formal documents.
8. **Unfinished work:** Review the TODO page and every highlighted placeholder. Remove completed work and clearly describe anything still missing.
9. **Usability:** Ask a trusted nontechnical person to find the immediate contacts, document location, and first actions without coaching.
10. **Offline and print:** Build a fresh copy, disconnect from the internet, test its links on another device, and inspect the full handbook in print preview.

Only after completing the applicable checks should the review date in `hugo.toml` be changed.

## Updating the review date

Edit the existing setting in `hugo.toml` and set `lastReviewed = "15 January 2026"`.

Use an unambiguous written month rather than a numeric date. Rebuild and redistribute the handbook after changing it.

## When to review

Review every 6–12 months and after any major change involving:

- family or dependants;
- health or personal wishes;
- employment or business ownership;
- housing, property, or vehicles;
- banking, investments, debt, insurance, or taxes;
- devices, phone numbers, email, or password management;
- executors, beneficiaries, nominees, guardians, or trusted contacts;
- relevant laws or a move to another jurisdiction.
