This section is for maintaining the protocol. It should make unfinished work visible without implying that a checked item is legally sufficient.

## Legal and authority

- [ ] Confirm who should act as executor or administrator and name a backup.
- [ ] Create or review a valid will with qualified local advice.
- [ ] Review guardianship, medical, and decision-making documents where relevant.
- [ ] Confirm that beneficiary and nominee designations are current and consistent.
- [ ] Record where originals are kept and who can access them.

## Information and access

- [ ] Complete the key-contact list.
- [ ] Create a current asset and liability inventory.
- [ ] Document recurring payments and household responsibilities.
- [ ] Configure and test password-manager emergency access.
- [ ] Organize recovery material without placing secrets in this site.
- [ ] Confirm that a trusted person can access the offline handbook.

## Insurance and resilience

- [ ] Review life, health, property, and liability coverage.
- [ ] Check whether emergency savings are accessible to the household.
- [ ] Record employer, retirement, and other death-related benefits.
- [ ] Identify gaps requiring professional advice or action.

## Test and review

- [ ] Replace every `[placeholder]` in the published handbook.
- [ ] Ask a trusted nontechnical person to find three important facts without help.
- [ ] Open `dist/index.html` on a different computer with Wi-Fi disabled.
- [ ] Test all links and print the urgent sections.
- [ ] Store copies in agreed locations.
- [ ] Set the next review date.

Suggested review frequency: every 6–12 months and after a major family, financial, legal, employment, or housing change.
