You may be tired, distracted, or dealing with several people at once. Please do not rely on memory. Use whatever is easiest: this printed form, a notebook, or a notes application you already know.

> **Nothing you type or mark on this website is saved.** Use it as a guide, then keep your actual notes somewhere appropriate.

## Before a call

- Check that the number, email, or website is an official route you trust.
- Know what you are asking for and whether they may need proof of authority.
- Share only what is needed for that conversation.
- Keep original documents unless there is a clear reason to hand one over.

Try to note:

- how you contacted them;
- what you told or sent them;
- any decision, deadline, promise, or cost;
- the name or reference they gave you; and
- who will do the next thing and when.
