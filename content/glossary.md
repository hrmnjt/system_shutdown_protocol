You do not need to read this page from beginning to end. It is here in case a word used elsewhere is unfamiliar. These are only plain-language explanations; the exact legal meaning may be different in the UAE and India or depend on our circumstances.

**Authorized representative during incapacity**

A person who may be able to act for someone who is alive but cannot make or communicate decisions. The authority needed, its limits, and whether a document is recognized must be verified for the applicable matter and jurisdiction.

**Beneficiary**

A person or organization designated to receive money, property, or another benefit. A designation does not necessarily answer who may administer or immediately access it.

**Coordinator**

The person organizing calls, records, care, and practical work. Coordination does not itself grant medical, financial, digital, or legal authority.

**Digital legacy or emergency access**

A provider-supported or documented process through which a previously chosen person may request access to specified accounts or data, sometimes after a waiting period.

**Estate**

The assets, rights, and obligations left by a person. The exact meaning and administration process vary by jurisdiction.

**Executor or estate administrator**

A person intended, appointed, or confirmed to administer matters after death. Intention in this handbook is not proof that the person has been legally appointed or may act immediately.

**Nominee**

A person named for a particular account or asset. Depending on the arrangement and applicable law, nomination, receipt, administration, and final ownership may not mean the same thing.

**Power of attorney or equivalent authority**

A formal arrangement that may authorize another person to act in specified matters while someone is alive. Its scope, activation, recognition, and end must be checked; it is not interchangeable with the executor role.

**Probate or estate-confirmation process**

A court, official, or legal process that may confirm a will or authorize estate administration. Requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction and circumstances.

**Protected inventory**

Encrypted files, a password manager, or protected physical records containing sensitive details excluded from this handbook.
