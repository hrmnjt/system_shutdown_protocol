Add terms that may be unfamiliar to the people using this guide. Definitions here are general and do not replace local legal advice.

**Beneficiary**  
A person or organization designated to receive money, property, or another benefit.

**Digital executor or legacy contact**  
A person designated, where a service or local law allows it, to handle aspects of a person’s digital accounts or data.

**Estate**  
The assets, rights, and obligations left by a person. The exact legal meaning and process vary by jurisdiction.

**Executor**  
A person appointed under a will to administer an estate, subject to local law and any required court authority.

**Nominee**  
A person named for a particular account or asset. Depending on local law, nomination may authorize receipt or administration without determining final ownership.

**Password-manager emergency access**  
A controlled process that lets a previously chosen trusted person request access, often after a waiting period.

**Probate**  
A court or legal process that may confirm a will or authorize estate administration. Requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction.
